What this tool does

It helps you decide where AI could apply in a marketing function. You choose the roles and activities that matter to you, and each activity carries a starting classification: full automation, augmentation (AI supports, a person decides) or human only. You can override any classification.

Using the planner

  1. Pick a role. Roles are in six groups, alphabetical within each group. Use Find a role to search. Tick a role to select all of its activities.
  2. Pick a task. Each role breaks into tasks. Select a task to see its activities.
  3. Select activities. Tick the activities that are part of the work you are assessing, or use Select all.
  4. Override if needed. The Default column shows the starting classification. Use the Override column to change it for your organisation.
  5. Add your own. Use + Add a role or + Add an activity if the starter list does not fit. Your own items get their own IDs and travel with your files.
  6. Check the Dashboard. It summarises your selection by role group and classification.

Save and export

On the Save and export tab, Download Results (XLSX) gives the Excel file that the AI Workflow Assessment and the AI Toolkit read. Save and Load let you carry on later. Reset clears everything.
